MISMATCH
The intent says a 10 GiB file (about 10.74×10⁹ bytes) fits in 10.5 GB of free disk, but the formal statement compares 8×10¹⁰ to 8.4×10¹⁰, which is a different pair of numbers (these look like bit counts, 10 GB×8 and 10.5 GB×8, rather than 10 GiB and 10.5 GB) and so does not capture the GiB-to-GB conversion that the claim depends on.
